These Battle covers are original raster compositions generated by
`scripts/generateCs2BattleArt.js` and `scripts/generateCs2BattleArt.py`.
Case silhouettes, typography, lighting and symbols are drawn in code. Skin cutouts
under `sources/` are the existing catalogue's Steam/Valve economy images; source
URLs and skin IDs are recorded in `catalogue.json`. CS2 skin artwork belongs to
its respective creators and Valve. No Skin.Club branding or artwork is included.

The bot sends the finished PNG files as local Discord attachments. It does not
need Python, an image service or access to the artwork source URLs at runtime.

Use `node scripts/generateCs2BattleArt.js --only-missing` to append artwork for
new catalogue cases while preserving existing covers and tie-breaker assets.
Use `--ids battle-p250,battle-five-seven` to rebuild specific covers. Both modes
refresh the full contact sheet; newly rendered covers also get a separate sheet.
Use `--selected-sheet contact-sheet-expansion-36.jpg` to keep an expansion's
preview separate from earlier batches. New gallery layouts use catalogue skin
cutouts with armory, technical, orbital, pedestal, neon and paired displays.
