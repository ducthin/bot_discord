These are genuine seed-specific pattern reference renders from pattern.wiki.
Their full original images, black backgrounds and provider watermarks are
preserved; PNG files only convert the image format. No skin was recolored.

`manifest.json` records the exact skin ID, paint seed, provider page, source URL,
original SHA-256 and PNG SHA-256 for each reference. The original WebP files are
under `sources/`. Rebuild or verify offline with:

    python scripts/generateCs2BlueGemArt.py

pattern.wiki documents a custom renderer without the game's lighting:
https://pattern.wiki/wiki/faq/
These images show the pattern placement. They do not depict a particular
simulated float, StatTrak counter, sticker configuration or exact game lighting.
Reference/provider attribution should remain visible when the bot displays them.

by pattern.wiki. The site's footer reserves its rights. The MIT notices for the
